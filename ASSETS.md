# Asset guidelines — How to make it truly photorealistic

## Not required for first demo
The package includes stylized original SVG placeholder jewelry rendered by CSS in `preview/index.html`. **These are illustrations and are not actual high-fidelity commercial product photography.** The MVP can use them (or better original placeholders) while the interaction is built.

## Real product asset checklist
For each of 3 categories, preferably provide:
- One high-resolution studio product shot (watch/ring/diamond), licensed for use, ideally transparent PNG/WebP with clean silhouette.
- Front or three-quarter view and optional side view.
- Box closed and box open photos at matching camera angles, or a textured 3D asset / GLB model with hinge topology.
- Product copy, verified materials/specifications, brand and rights to display.

## Art direction for future shots
- Ring: frontal three-quarter gold or platinum band, mounted gemstone, sharp controlled highlights; velvet espresso box.
- Watch: centered watch on cushion, dark brushed-metal/leather environment; crisp dial, no invented brand insignia.
- Diamond: isolated faceted gem with balanced refractive sparkle, on ivory satin/stone display.
- Keep a consistent horizon/perspective and light direction so box motion and the revealed object belong to the same world.

## Photography vs actual 3D
- Layered PNG/WebP + CSS hinged lid: easiest, fast and often visually convincing, but limited to fixed camera positions.
- GLB + Three.js: supports true perspective changes and material lighting, but requires proper modeling, UV, lighting, optimized geometry/textures and careful mobile fallback. **Do not promise realistic 3D without real 3D source assets.**

## Image sourcing
Use user-supplied and properly licensed images. Do not copy images from arbitrary luxury brands or present fake branded watches. Create original demo illustrations or free-to-use assets with verified licenses.

## Missing info before public launch
Real brand name/logo, exact goods, verified product photos, contact email/phone/WhatsApp, privacy policy needs, and optionally domain.
