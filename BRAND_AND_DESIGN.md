# Creative Brief — Jewelry Box Reveal

## Project concept
A one-page interactive editorial presentation that turns discovery of jewelry into a small ceremonial moment. The design is inspired by the minimal, highly interactive fashion-rack storefront, but replaces the rack with three luxury cases. NOT a conventional full store, product grid or generic business template.

## Working name
**MAISON ÉCLAT / FINE OBJECTS** — placeholder only; brand/domain/trademark not checked. All text and logo must be easy to replace via a central config.

## Audience
Visitors considering premium gifts, special occasions, jewelry and luxury watches; they may be browsing on phones. Make it welcoming and understandable rather than intimidating or excessively opulent.

## Emotional arc
**Curiosity** (three closed cases) → **Interaction** (tap one) → **Reveal** (object and lighting) → **Desire** (short editorial copy) → **Action** (ask a question or schedule a consultation).

## Typography
Large refined Latin serif (Georgia fallback) for editorial accent / numbers; Hebrew should use a legible contemporary sans or suitable Hebrew serif. Keep real Hebrew legibility paramount. Don't depend on an unavailable paid font. Use CSS font stack or a properly licensed public font; no font files in repo unless legally licensed.

## Layout
- Top bar: reserved wordmark, 2–3 anchors, contact link.
- Hero: on desktop a spacious editorial message balanced with 3 cases on a subtle stage. Cases visually distinct and share an aligned baseline.
- On mobile, a compact 3-item stage or accessible swipeable scene; clear hint to touch.
- Selected category detail row directly below stage; dynamic text is not hidden under the fold where possible.
- One secondary section: philosophy/craftsmanship/curation, avoiding unverified historic origin story.
- Contact area and footer. Avoid eight redundant sections.

## Motion direction
A choreography, not a slideshow: short anticipation, box opens, object receives attention, a tiny reflection appears, category details fade in. Target perceptually smooth interaction; adjust timings by visual QA. Motion feels luxurious from easing, light, and physical connection—not overlong waiting.

## Non-goals
- Full cart and checkout in MVP.
- Real inventory or pricing.
- Claims about metal purity, natural/lab diamonds, provenance or certification without verified source data.
- Complex 3D engine when CSS layers can achieve visual quality.
